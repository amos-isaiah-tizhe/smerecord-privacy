The Problem

Running a business without records is flying blind

The notebook gets lost
You write it down today, you can't find it next week. Rain ruins it. Pages tear. And you never know the total without adding everything up by hand.
"I think I made profit this month"
At the end of every month you're not sure if the business made money. Goods are moving but somehow there's never enough cash left.
WhatsApp messages as receipts
Payment screenshots, invoices, delivery notes, buried in different chats. There's no single place to see the full financial picture.
Banks want records you don't have
When you need a loan or want to expand, they ask for financial records. You have nothing to show. The opportunity passes you by.
